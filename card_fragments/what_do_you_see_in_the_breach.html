What Do You See in the Breach

card variation

Sabotage

Success Criteria

Play the card at night. The virus infects your opponent's capital. You now have 2 days where you can take it before capturing the rest of your opponent's territories.

Successful Sabotage Result

You take your opponent's capital within the 3 days time period. This does not end the game. You now have another capital and your opponent does not. They must take all other territories before attacking either capital.

Failure Consequence

You fail to take the capital within 3 days. Any souls still in the capital after the time period become imprisoned. After 10 diel cycles, your opponent chooses up to 2 souls to take under their control, they release 1 of the remaining souls to you, then your opponent wastes the rest.
