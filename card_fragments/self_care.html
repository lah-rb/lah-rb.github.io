Can heal 1 keal means damage from all souls with in the same forest movement space (not base camp or capital) during a day diel cycle roll. All healed souls must rest (take no actions) until the next day is rolled. If the souls have already taken actions this day they are not eligible to receive this effect. Self Care cannot take other actions the day this effect is used. Self Care can engage in keal means bouts, but if she wins in lue of damage, she transports the soul to a different forest movement space on the board (player's choice). She can only be engaged in final bouts.
