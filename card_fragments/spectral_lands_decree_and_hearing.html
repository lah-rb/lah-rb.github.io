Spectral Land's Decree and Hearing

card variation

Sabotage

Success Criteria

Roll 2 D6, any set of doubles or opposites means success:
1-1, 1-6, 2-2, 2-5, 3-3, 3-4, 4-4, 5-5, or 6-6 order does not matter.

Successful Sabotage Result

The spectral lands side with you. Choose three territories that can't be taken in the next 3 turns.

Failure Consequence

The spectral lands side with your opponent. Your opponent chooses 2 territories that can't be taken in the next 2 turns.

Scarcity

Founder's Deck

History

The solemn court is held. The perpetrator knows of the wrong they are accused of and knows the conviction of there heart. This is not a court of people, this is a court of a king. The subject feels the weight and power of the king rock from side, to side as they decern with perfect judgement the fate of the accused. 'I hold the high seat, tasked to protect these lands. I feel the spirit of all those who tread upon me. Your guilt is not a question, but your omission stands as such. So tell me, what crimes against this kingdom will you admit to?'
