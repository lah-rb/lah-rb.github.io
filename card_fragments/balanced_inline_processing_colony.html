History

Biochemists, tasked by a group of descendants in Indochina, designed a bacterium capable of transmitting electric signals around the cell wall to their colony through the cell's pilus (hair-like appendages). These were then coxed into bacterial arrays capable of enduring higher currents. All of this was with the intent of producing full-fledged micro-processors capable of storing data without the use of any metal in order to smuggle secrets into communist China. The first iteration of the project, though prohibitively expensive, proved successful without the governments determining foul play. The agents responsible for delivering the devices proved to be the weak point of the process. Many got caught, due to repeat travel, while others were incapable maintaining the conditions sufficient for the chip's survival. Due to the touchy nature of the chip's survival, data was never captured and the project lived on. Some wonder if these colonies are capable of self awareness.
