History

The Myrthvither Raven is a constantly moving and easily adapts to new environments. It possesses a wicked intelligence and tends to be quite self serving. Often, these ravens know several languages and can effectively communicate between species. Imbued with foliage near death, they easily disguise themselves in natural environments. They act as nearly perfect scouts, if offered a strong enough incentive to put themselves at risk. Many request exotic ant species as payment. Their intent with this behavior is unclear, however, some have observed ants living within their plant mass, a likely sign of a symbiotic relationship.
