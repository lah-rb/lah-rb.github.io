Avian Keeper's Den

card variation

Terrain Feature

game effects

Acts as a prison for non-avian creatures. In order to imprison a soul, they must have only 1 injury before defeat and be in a territory touching the territory this feature is within. In order to take the prisoner, you must engage the enemy soul in a keal means bout with a card whose archetypal gene is avian. The card will be kept in prison until the territory is captured by the opposing player. Set the card aside out of play while imprisoned.

Scarcity

Founder's Deck

History

Coming to the end of the gate, a restless explorer peers out to discover a grand building built on what appears to be a massive floating rock. Upon further inspection, they notice a stream of flying creatures approach the ledge at the command of the inhabitants of the palace. The explorer watches the keepers bring their beasts in and travel out on their backs for hours. They wonder just who built this and what greater purpose it serves.
