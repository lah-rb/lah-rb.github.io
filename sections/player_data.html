Player Data

Your game progress is kept on this device and nowhere else: the damage on your cards, your timers, your affinity, and each card's loyalty. From here you can carry it to another device, or keep a copy of it.

Sync a device

Carry a game on from one device to another. The device you start on shows a code, and the other device scans it. The game in progress on the other device is replaced. Your record is merged: each card's loyalty is added together, so nothing either device knows is lost. If you are in a room with another player, your place in it goes with the game, and the other device carries on there.

The two devices talk through our relay, which cannot read what they say: it is encrypted with a key that only the code carries.

Backup

A backup is a file, locked with a passphrase you choose. Keep one before you clear this browser's data, or to move to a device you cannot have beside this one.
