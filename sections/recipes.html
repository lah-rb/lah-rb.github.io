Recipe Lookup

About the lookup

In Kipukas, you can craft recipes from base items. This page details all the recipes for the first 5 item combinations in the founder's decks. Just select the items that you want to craft and a recipe will appear! We made this page to showcase the game mechanic, particularly how flexible it behaves. For competition purposes, these recipes will always be valid to use. As the game matures, we plan on giving out recipes with each order. These will be kept in each player's personal recipe book and they will only be allowed to play recipes from within their books for competitive play.
